Blueberry Sour Cream
Tart

Ukrainian-style · Whole wheat crust · Frozen berries · Reduced sugar

25 min
~55–70 min
2+ hrs
28cm tart · 10–12 slices

Ingredients

Crust

260gWhole wheat flour
180gCold unsalted butter, cubed
40gSugar
1Egg yolk
½ tspVanilla powder
1 pinchFine salt
3–5 tbspIce water, as needed

Blueberry layer

400gFrozen blueberries, unthawed
2½ tbspPotato starch

Sour cream custard

550gTurkish yogurt 10%, strained
3Eggs
70–80gSugar
2 tbspCornstarch
1 tspVanilla powder

Method

  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour, sugar and salt until sandy. Add the egg yolk and vanilla, then bring together with ice water a tablespoon at a time — whole wheat dough needs more water than white flour, add until it just holds without feeling wet. Chill 30–40 minutes.

  2. Roll out and line the 28cm tin, patching any cracks with your fingers rather than re-rolling. Chill 15 minutes, then blind bake at 180°C with weights for 12–15 minutes, then 5 minutes without, until pale gold.

  3. Toss the frozen blueberries with the potato starch while still frozen, and scatter evenly over the baked base.

  4. Whisk the strained yogurt, eggs, sugar, cornstarch and vanilla powder until smooth, then pour over the blueberries.

  5. Bake at 160°C for 40–55 minutes — longer than usual since the fruit is frozen — until the custard is set with only a slight wobble in the center.

  6. Cool completely, at least 2 hours, before slicing. It firms up further as it cools.

Whole wheat crust — it absorbs more liquid and browns faster than white flour, so the extra butter and water above are intentional. Rest the dough the full 30–40 minutes; the gluten is more active and the dough needs time to relax before rolling.

Frozen blueberries — used straight from the freezer, never thawed, to avoid a watery filling. The extra starch (2½ tbsp vs. the usual 1½) compensates for the extra liquid they release during baking.
