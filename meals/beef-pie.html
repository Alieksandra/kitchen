Quick Whole Wheat
Beef Pie

Press-in crust · Single crust · No onion

15 min
25–30 min
~45 min
4 servings

Ingredients

Crust

200g Whole wheat flour
100g Bregott laktosfri, cold, cubed
60ml Cold water
Pinch Salt

Filling

500g Ground beef
2 Garlic cloves, minced
200ml Stock (beef or vegetable)
1 tbsp Whole wheat flour
1½ tsp Dried thyme
To taste Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C fan. Butter your dish well, including the edges — a 28cm glass dish works, though the pie will sit thinner and the crust-to-filling ratio will be higher than in a smaller dish.

  2. Rub the cold cubed butter into the flour and salt until crumbly. Stir in the cold water until it just comes together, then press evenly into the base and up the sides of the dish with your fingers. No resting needed.

  3. Heat a pan over medium-high heat. Brown the ground beef, breaking it apart as it cooks, about 6–7 minutes.

  4. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.

  5. Stir in the flour, then add the stock. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened, about 8 minutes.

  6. Season with thyme, salt, and pepper.

  7. Spoon the filling into the crust and spread evenly.

  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the crust is golden. In a 28cm glass dish, start checking a few minutes early — glass retains heat and the thinner filling layer cooks faster.

  9. Let rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Tips — No onion or carrot here, so garlic and thyme do the flavour work — don't skip either. With no resting step, the press-in crust is more delicate than a rolled one; press firmly and evenly, especially up the sides.

Quantities are sized for a standard ~22–24cm dish. In a wider 28cm dish, expect a thinner pie with more exposed crust — reduce bake time slightly if it colours fast.
