Frozen Desserts — Solo Scoops

Fresh Mint &
Vanilla Ice Cream

Steeped fresh mint · Low sugar · No egg · Cuisinart Solo Scoops

15 min
2 hrs+
~20 min
~250g

Ingredients

60gWhole milk
15gFresh mint leaves, bruised
20gGranulated sugar
10gGlucose syrup
165gValio whipping cream
¼ tspVanilla powder
1 pinchFine salt
2 tabletsHermesetas (optional)

Method

  1. Warm the milk gently in a small pan until just steaming — do not boil. Remove from heat, stir in the bruised mint leaves, cover, and steep 20–30 minutes.

  2. Strain out the mint, pressing the leaves against the sieve to extract all the infused milk. Whisk in the sugar, vanilla powder, salt and crushed Hermesetas (if using) until fully dissolved. Warm the glucose syrup briefly if too stiff to pour, then stir it in.

  3. Stir in the cold Valio cream. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.

  4. Turn the Solo Scoops ON first, then pour the chilled base through the funnel. Churn ~20 minutes until thickened.

  5. Transfer to an airtight container. Freeze 2 hours for a firmer scoop. Rest 10–15 min before serving.

For a smoother texture — glucose syrup is doing some of the work egg yolk normally would. It softens the texture without adding much extra sweetness, so it pairs well with a mint flavour that shouldn't taste candy-sweet.

Mint flavour — steeping bruised leaves in warm milk gives a rounder, softer mint flavour than muddling them cold. For a sharper, more assertive mint, steep closer to 30 minutes and press firmly when straining.

Colour — this stays a pale, natural cream colour rather than bright green, since no food colouring or spinach is used. The vanilla powder is a background note here — mint is the lead flavour.
